### Paths

Every way the draft can end, as the checker enumerates it: each path shows whether it succeeds, its entry, and the amr and acr a succeeding path issues. Select one to highlight it in the blocks and the graph; filter by outcome in the toolbar.

The path budget at the top shows, per entry, how many paths it has against the checker's limit of 10,000. The bar is green under half, yellow to three quarters and red past that. Paths multiply with every alternative, condition and retry, so a red entry has little room left. An entry over the limit is an error: the draft cannot be published until it is split.
